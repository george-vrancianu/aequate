[REUSE.toml](REUSE.toml) says which license covers which file. The reasons are in [ADR 0007](docs/adr/0007-agpl-with-dco-no-dual-licensing.md).

## External contributions are on hold

We don't merge pull requests from outside contributors yet. The licensing decision is waiting on a lawyer's review ([#55](https://github.com/george-vrancianu/aequate/issues/55)), and merging outside code before then would close options we still need. Issues and discussion are welcome.

## Developer Certificate of Origin

Every commit must be signed off under the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/): add `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` with your real name (`git commit -s`). By signing off you certify that you have the right to submit the change under the license of the files it touches. You keep your copyright. There is no CLA, and Aequate is not dual-licensed.

## Code written with AI agents

Commits made with an AI agent carry a `Co-Authored-By` trailer that names the agent. The human who signs off takes responsibility for the change, as for any other code they submit.
