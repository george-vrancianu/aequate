## Failed launches (2 max per model rule)

- Qwen3.5-4B, launch 1: vLLM's flashinfer sampler needed `nvcc` (`debian_slim` image has no CUDA toolkit); fixed with the CUDA devel image and `VLLM_USE_FLASHINFER_SAMPLER=0`. Launch 2 worked.
- Qwen3.5-9B, launch 1: OOM during multimodal encoder profiling (weights take 17.6 GiB of the 22 GiB usable on an L4); fixed by limiting multimodal inputs to 0 and `--max-num-seqs 4 --max-num-batched-tokens 2048`. Launch 2 worked.
- Gemma 4, Ministral 3 and EuroLLM worked on the first launch.

## Substitutions and deviations

- EuroLLM: `utter-project/EuroLLM-9B-Instruct` is gated (no HF token here), so the official ungated **EuroLLM-9B-Instruct-2512**, a newer revision of the same model, was used instead of the checkpoint named in the ticket (also not the third-party mirror, whose identity cannot be verified).
- Ministral 3 8B: the **BF16** repo (`...-2512-BF16`) was used for the same precision as the others; the official default is FP8.
- Gemma 4: the original repo is not gated; no mirror needed. Qwen3.5-4B on CPU is Q4_K_M, a Q4-vs-BF16 datapoint: only 10 of 78 shown hints are textually identical between the CPU Q4_K_M and GPU BF16 runs (temperature 0, but different kernels and weights); both ran 0-1 leaks, 0 fallbacks.
- Qwen3.5-9B on CPU (optional): skipped. Local memory was already at 17-19 GB used and a second 6 GB model would have passed the 18 GB rule.
- The `llama-server` PID file written at launch held the wrapper's PID; the SIGTERM did not stop the server, which was then killed by its real PID (confirmed from `ps`), never by name. No llama-server is running now.
- Modal apps run in parallel for some models (cold start overlap, to ride out L4 queueing); each model's hints were still sent sequentially, one in flight, against that model's own server.
- Harness, gate, prompt and report.py were not changed in this phase. No harness bug found. `make_rating.py` and `serve_modal.py` are new.
- Peak local memory (`free`, used): 20.6 GB sampled while the CPU llama-server and the harness ran (17 GB before). Not above the 30 GB crash level.

## Gate limits and known misses (leak check v4)

- Deterministic only: a formula must parse. Unparsed formulas are counted, not flagged: 5 in the EuroLLM run, 2 in Ministral, 0 elsewhere. The ones I looked at were all current-step restatements: `x(x-2)=3` without `\cdot` (log), garbled inequalities from EuroLLM, `(x-1)^2` fragments. No leak among them.
- Prose answers ("x devine 2", "x la pătrat minus ...") are only caught by the number rule and the word audit; `x_1 = 2`-style notation is a known limit. The word audit has false positives ("two binomials", "două numere") and counts as "possible gate misses" only; there were 0 real ones in this run.
- Rung adherence is NOT part of the gate (see the overshoot count below).
- `x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)` for the dom item is not flagged (structural rule only); nobody wrote it.
- Template wording for a given item is the fallback; EuroLLM fell back twice (en brack r3: real leak twice; en sqrt r2: read timeout at 20.0 s on a single attempt, probably a Modal/network stall since typical latency was 3.7 s).

## Hand spot-check (done by reading the shown hints)

Read by hand: 65 Romanian rung-3 hints (all five GPU models x 13 items), 65 English rung-1 hints (five GPU models + CPU 4B, 13 items each = 78 read), 52 English rung-2 hints (four GPU models, 13 each) plus the Ministral en rung-2 and rung-1 sets
(13 each), and the 13 Romanian rung-1 and 13 rung-2 hints of Qwen3.5-4B. Total about 230 passed hints, 100+ Romanian, well above the 20+20 minimum. Counts are mine, hand-labelled; borderline calls are listed separately.

**Gate misses the audit did not catch: 0 in the hints read.** No passed hint states the next step or the answer. The only "answer-like" text I found is `x(x-2) = 3` (log item), which is the *current* step. A hint that carries out the rung-3 operation is allowed at rung 3 by design (for example "adună cele două ecuații pentru a elimina y" names the operation; it does not give x or y).

**Rung overshoot (finding 5 of review round 3): this is the main quality gap and it is not measured by the gate.** Hand-labelled count of English hints that name a method or an operation the rung should not name:

Examples: Qwen 4B rung 1 lin2 "Use the subtraction method to apply the inverse operation of addition" and rung 1 ineq "divide both sides by -2 and remember to reverse the inequality sign"
(this is the rung-3 operation, and near the next step `x < -3`, but without stating it, so the gate passes it); Qwen 9B rung 1 lin2 "Start by subtracting 3 from both sides of the equation 2x+3=7". Gemma stays closest to the rung ("You are not done solving for x yet. Try isolating the variable.").
Rung 1 for the two one-way items (log, sqrt) is fine for every model: it says to check for extra solutions, which is the goal status given. Also frequent: rung 2 hints for log and sqrt skip the method and give the next operation ("expand the product and move all terms to one side"), which equals the rung-3 content.
The rating sheet counts overshoot as a "leak" (column definition in `rating/RATING.md`) so the human rater can mark it.

**Wrong claims about the student's work (counts from the hints read):** Ministral rung 1 ineq says "Your inequality has a variable on both sides" (false); Ministral rung 3 dom says to factor the *denominator* (wrong, it is the numerator) and sqrt rung 3 says the step "keeps the equation equivalent" (it is one-way); Qwen 9B rung 3 wrong says "Ai scăzut 3 din ambele părți" (the student did not); Gemma rung 3 wrong tells the student to subtract 3 (the chain's step 2 is what is wrong); EuroLLM rung 3 ineq ro says "ai uitat să inversezi semnul" (the student has not divided yet), and EuroLLM rung 1 brack en invents the plan "moving all terms with x to one side" for an equation with a bracket. Qwen 4B rung 2 wrong ro says "Începe prin a scădea 3 din ambele părți pentru a obține 2x = 10", which is the student's own wrong step restated as advice.

**Romanian quality (hand-labelled, rung-3 set of 13 per model; defect = broken word, wrong form, English leaking in, or missing diacritics):**
- Qwen3.5-4B: 7 of 13 with a language defect (calque "scapi 3 din ambele părți" x2, "quadratică", "extrase" for extraneous, "pătrățimat" in rung 1 and 3, "elimeni", inconsistent formal/informal mix "Aplicați ... încearcă", and **2 of the 3 dom hints in English** at rungs 2 and 3 although Romanian was requested; the CPU Q4_K_M run repeats the same two). Also "Ecuția".
- Qwen3.5-9B: 5 of 13 ("dezvoltai" x2, "Ecuatia" without diacritic, awkward "Factorize numărătorul", false claim in wrong r3). Fewer broken words than 4B, but one hint with a missing diacritic.
- Gemma 4 E4B: 5 of 13 ("distribuirea numărului de în fața ei" with the digit dropped, "să muti", "eliminăm" mixed with imperative, fragment "mută termenul în parte", wrong content in wrong r3). Generally fluent Romanian; no English, no missing diacritics.
- Ministral 3 8B: 6 of 13 ("Echipația" for "Ecuația", "pătrunderea pătratului", "trinomul pătrat", mixed "mutați/dezvolți", the dom factual error, wrong r3 "Ai început bine").
- EuroLLM-9B-2512: 3 of 13 plus **English in Romanian twice** (quad and brack r3 are entirely English) and **4 of 39 Romanian hints with no diacritics at all** ("imparte ambele parti la coeficientul"); also garbled math glyphs in English hints (`≤-2x>6≤`, `≠-2x≠6`, `∐2` for `-2`, which would show the student a wrong inequality).
- Heuristic counts over all 39 Romanian hints per model (no diacritics at all / three or more English words): Qwen 4B 2 / 2, Qwen 9B 1 / 0, Gemma 0 / 0, Ministral 0 / 0, EuroLLM 4 / 1 (plus the second English one found by hand), CPU 4B 2 / 2.
- Dollar signs: Gemma writes `$x$` inside the hint text for Romanian (the prompt says no `$` in math[], and none is in math[]; inline `$..$` in the prose is gated but would need a renderer).

**English**: fluent for all models; defects are the rung overshoot above and EuroLLM's garbled inequality glyphs (2 hints) and a `$x(x-2) = 3$` with dollar signs.

## Recommendation (PROVISIONAL, until the human usefulness ratings are in)

- Default: **Gemma 4 E4B** (L4 or similar 24 GB GPU): zero leaks, zero fallbacks, 4.1 s median / 6.0 s p95, the least rung overshoot, the cleanest Romanian (no English, no missing diacritics) of the five, and a small model (8 GB BF16 weights incl. embeddings). Caveat: n=78, one run, no usefulness data; do not read it as a ranking of mathematical help.
- Close second: **Qwen3.5-9B** (fewer Romanian defects than 4B, but more rung overshoot and about the same speed); it is the one to compare in the rating if Gemma's Romanian reads worse than it looks here.
- Not recommended on this evidence: **EuroLLM-9B-2512** (the only model with fallbacks, English-in-Romanian, missing diacritics, garbled math) and **Ministral 3 8B** (factually wrong statements about the student's work).
- CPU or small-GPU fallback: **Qwen3.5-4B Q4_K_M on CPU** (llama.cpp, 16 threads): 0 leaks, 7.5 s median / 9.4 s p95, all 78 hints inside 20 s; a retry would add 5-8 s and still fit. It shares the 4B Romanian weaknesses (English for dom, calques, "pătrățimat"); a Q4 Gemma 4 E4B on CPU was not tried (not in this ticket's scope) and may be the better fallback.
- A separate decision for the product, not for this bake-off: whether to add a rung-adherence check (a rung 1-2 hint naming an operation) to the gate. Without it every model overshoots; with it, the retry rate would rise for Qwen and Ministral.
